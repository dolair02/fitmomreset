Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026 • Effective Date: September 27, 2026

At Fit Mom Reset ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Dolair, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users, particularly busy mothers navigating postpartum health and wellness. This Privacy Policy explains how our iOS mobile application ("App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information in compliance with the Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect information strictly necessary to provide you with personalized workouts, core recovery tracking, and account management:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your name and email address to create your profile, secure your account, and manage password resets.
  • User-Generated Health & Wellness Logs: Data you choose to log inside the app, including daily habit checks, water intake (64 oz tracker), sleep energy battery ratings, and voluntary Diastasis Recti finger-gap measurements (1.0 to 4.0 fingers). This data is stored securely and is never sold.
  • Subscription & Purchase Data: Subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's StoreKit in-app purchase infrastructure. We never collect, store, or have access to your credit card or financial details.

2. What We DO NOT Collect or Share

🛡️ Zero Third-Party Advertising & Zero Data Brokers

• We do NOT track you across other companies' apps or websites (Zero IDFA tracking).

• We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal, fitness, or postpartum health data to any data brokers, ad networks, or third parties.

• We do NOT collect location data, biometric data, or microphone audio recordings.

3. Audio & Media Permissions

The App includes audio countdown beeps for 15-minute circuits and authentic Tibetan singing bowl audio for guided 4-7-8 breathing exercises. These audio files are bundled locally within the app and use the standard iOS AVAudioSessionCategoryPlayback session. No audio is recorded, captured, or transmitted from your device.

4. How to Delete Your Account (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

In full compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, every user has the absolute right to delete their account and all associated data at any time:

Option A (In-App): Navigate to your profile or Admin settings and tap "Delete My Account".

Option B (Email Support): Send an email from your registered address to Dolair02@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Account". All associated profile data, habit logs, and credentials will be permanently erased within 48 hours.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Fit Mom Reset is designed for adult mothers and caregivers aged 18 and older. While the app features toddler feeding tips, it is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
