Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my VIP Membership or 7-Day Free Trial?

All subscriptions and free trials are managed safely and securely by Apple through your Apple ID:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Fit Mom Reset and tap Cancel Subscription.

You will retain full access until the end of your current billing period with zero cancellation fees.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

If you upgrade your iPhone or reinstall the app, open the Fit Mom Reset VIP Modal and tap "Restore Purchases". The app will immediately verify your active subscription with Apple's App Store servers at no charge.

How does the Diastasis Recti Clinic work?

Our clinical self-check guides you through the gentle 2-finger check to test for separation above, at, and below the navel. When you switch on "Diastasis-Safe Mode" inside any workout, the app automatically swaps high-intra-abdominal-pressure moves (like sit-ups and front planks) for pelvic-floor safe 360 diaphragmatic zips.

What is the "Pause for Baby" feature?

Real life happens! If your baby cries or needs a bottle during a 15-minute circuit, simply tap the "Pause for Baby" button. Your timer pauses without shame or penalty, and soft audio chimes reassure you to take all the time you need.
